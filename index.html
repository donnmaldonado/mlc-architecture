About us

A full-service architectural firm based in Patchogue, New York

MLC Architecture PC brings its professional expertise to clients across Long Island and New York City. Founded on the belief of delivering customized solutions, MLC offers an efficient design process that seamlessly integrates all project elements for residential and commercial clients.

Our clients’ lives are enriched and enhanced by these designs that are timeless, functional, and aesthetic.
