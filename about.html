About us

MLC Architecture PC, a full-service architectural firm based in Patchogue, New York, brings its professional expertise to clients across Long Island and New York City. Founded on the belief of delivering customized solutions, MLC offers an efficient design process that seamlessly integrates all project elements for residential and commercial clients.

Our clients’ lives are enriched and enhanced by these designs that are timeless, functional, and aesthetic.

Our mission

Our mission

At MLC Architecture PC, we are passionate about design’s ability to transform lives. Through innovation, sustainability, and close collaboration, we create inspiring, functional, and enduring spaces for residential and commercial clients.
